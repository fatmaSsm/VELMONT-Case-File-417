## The Case

**March 4, 1997 — Velmont East Harbor.**

Victor Hale, 47, is found dead beneath **Cargo Pier No. 3** just before dawn.

The coroner calls it **homicide**.  
The harbor master insists it was an **accident**.  
The newspapers report a possible **suicide**.

On your desk are seven statements, three documents, and a crime scene.

But you cannot arrest anyone — **not without evidence.**

---

## Investigation

🔦 Explore a dark harbor crime scene using your flashlight

🗣️ Question **7 suspects** and uncover contradictions in their statements

📰 Examine documents, newspaper clippings, phone records, maps, and timelines

🔎 Connect evidence and separate real clues from misleading ones

🎭 Reach **5 different endings** depending on your investigation and decisions

🌍 Play in **Turkish or English**

## About the Project

VELMONT — Case File #417 is an interactive detective game developed from concept to release under **PicoBird Studios**.

The project focuses on investigation, environmental storytelling, player observation, and evidence-based decision making.

Instead of simply choosing dialogue options, players are expected to examine the case, recognize contradictions, and justify their final accusation with evidence.
